XUBO Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XUBO handles information when you use the application.

Important: XUBO is a media player. XUBO does not provide, sell, host, or redistribute IPTV subscriptions, television channels, movies, series, or other media content.

1. About XUBO

XUBO allows users to connect to their own IPTV or media service. Users are responsible for obtaining access to their own compatible media service and for ensuring that they are authorized to access the content provided by that service.

2. Information You Provide

To connect to a user-selected IPTV or media service, XUBO may process information entered by the user, including:

  • Server or provider URL
  • Username
  • Password
  • Profile name

This information is used only to establish and maintain the connection to the service selected by the user and to provide the application's requested functionality.

3. Local Application Data

XUBO may store application data locally on the user's device in order to provide its functionality.

Depending on the features used, locally stored information may include:

  • Saved profiles
  • Provider or server information
  • Login credentials
  • Favorites
  • Watch history
  • Playback progress and resume information
  • Application preferences and settings

This information is used to provide application functionality and continuity of the user's local experience.

XUBO does not operate a central XUBO account system for storing this information.

4. Authentication Credentials

Credentials entered into XUBO are used to authenticate directly with the IPTV or media service configured by the user.

XUBO does not sell, rent, or use these credentials for advertising or marketing purposes.

Credentials may be stored locally on the device using platform-provided secure storage mechanisms where applicable.

5. Communication With Third-Party Services

When a user configures a media provider, XUBO communicates with that provider over the network in order to perform functions such as:

  • Authenticating the user's account
  • Retrieving categories and media listings
  • Retrieving electronic program guide information when available
  • Retrieving movie and series metadata when provided
  • Loading artwork, posters, logos, and related resources
  • Requesting and playing media streams selected by the user

Information required to perform these requests, including provider credentials where required by the provider's API, may be transmitted directly to the service configured by the user.

Media providers may also return links to external content delivery, artwork, metadata, or media servers. XUBO may connect to those servers as necessary to display or play content requested by the user.

These third-party services operate independently of XUBO and may have their own privacy policies, security practices, and data retention policies.

6. Analytics and Advertising

XUBO does not operate its own advertising network.

XUBO does not sell personal information to advertisers.

XUBO does not use user credentials, viewing activity, favorites, or playback history for targeted advertising.

XUBO does not intentionally transmit this information to the publisher for marketing purposes.

7. Media Content

Media listings and streams displayed by XUBO originate from services configured by the user.

XUBO does not control the content, availability, privacy practices, or security practices of those services.

Users should review the privacy policy and terms of their selected IPTV or media provider for information about how that provider processes their information.

8. Data Sharing

XUBO does not sell personal information.

XUBO does not intentionally disclose personal information to unrelated third parties.

Information may be transmitted to the user's selected provider, and where necessary to servers referenced by that provider, only as required to provide functionality requested by the user.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Application information stored by XUBO is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Users can remove saved profiles and associated application data through the application's available profile management functionality.

Users may also remove locally stored application data by uninstalling or resetting the application through the operating system.

Information retained independently by an IPTV or media provider is controlled by that provider and must be managed through that provider.

10. Security

XUBO is designed to use platform security mechanisms and secure storage facilities where applicable.

Network security also depends on the server and connection method provided by the user's selected media service.

Users should use trusted service providers and secure HTTPS connections whenever supported by their provider.

11. Children's Privacy

XUBO is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

XUBO does not provide media content itself. The availability and suitability of media accessible through a configured provider is determined by that provider and the user's account.

Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising the services configured for use with XUBO where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when XUBO features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available at this Privacy Policy URL.
